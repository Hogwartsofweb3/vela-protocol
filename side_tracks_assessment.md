# Frontier Hackathon Side Track Assessment for Vela Protocol

This document evaluates the 8 side tracks provided to determine their strategic viability for Vela Protocol (RWA Yield Aggregator). 

## S-TIER (Perfect Matches — You MUST apply for these)

### 1. SuperteamNG x Raenest Side Track
- **URL:** [superteam.fun/earn/listing/superteamng-x-raenest-colosseum-frontier-hackathon-side-track](https://superteam.fun/earn/listing/superteamng-x-raenest-colosseum-frontier-hackathon-side-track)
- **Why it fits:** You are a Nigerian founder, and Raenest builds high-velocity financial infrastructure for African businesses. Vela fits beautifully here as a yield-bearing backend. You can pitch Vela as: *"An institutional yield layer that Raenest can plug into to offer their business clients 5% APY on idle USD balances."*
- **Verdict:** Highly recommended. Easiest narrative fit.

### 2. $50,000 in Security Audit Credits by Adevar Labs Inc.
- **URL:** [superteam.fun/earn/listing/50k-adevarlabs-bounty](https://superteam.fun/earn/listing/50k-adevarlabs-bounty)
- **Why it fits:** Vela handles user funds, vaults, and Token-2022 transfer hooks. It is a DeFi application, meaning it *must* be audited before mainnet. Winning audit credits is incredibly valuable for a DeFi startup and shows judges you care about security.
- **Verdict:** Highly recommended. 

### 3. Build with Torque MCP
- **URL:** [superteam.fun/earn/listing/build-with-torque-mcp-1](https://superteam.fun/earn/listing/build-with-torque-mcp-1)
- **Why it fits:** Our entire hackathon narrative is **"Agentic Founding."** You are using me (an AI agent with MCP skills) to build this protocol. Torque is an on-chain execution protocol for AI agents. We can easily wire Torque to act as the "Keeper" that automatically calls the `rebalance()` instruction on Vela.
- **Verdict:** Highly recommended. Perfectly matches our team's AI-driven DNA.

## C-TIER (Ignore — Does Not Fit Vela)

### 6. Privacy Track (MagicBlock, ST MY & SNS)
- **URL:** [superteam.fun/earn/listing/privacy-track-colosseum-hackathon-powered-by-magicblock-st-my-and-sns](https://superteam.fun/earn/listing/privacy-track-colosseum-hackathon-powered-by-magicblock-st-my-and-sns)
- **Why it misses:** MagicBlock is for ephemeral rollups (games) and SNS is for domain names. Trying to cram privacy mechanics into an institutional RWA yield aggregator is a massive distraction.
- **Verdict:** Skip. Focus your time on the core program.

### 7. Build with Umbra Side Track
- **URL:** [superteam.fun/earn/listing/umbra-side-track](https://superteam.fun/earn/listing/umbra-side-track)
- **Why it misses:** Umbra is a stealth address protocol. Integrating stealth deposits for an institutional RWA platform actually introduces massive compliance / AML red flags (see the previous lesson on "Red Flags"). 
- **Verdict:** Skip. It breaks our institutional compliance narrative.

### 8. 100xDevs Frontier Hackathon Track
- **URL:** [superteam.fun/earn/listing/100xdevs-frontier-hackathon-track](https://superteam.fun/earn/listing/100xdevs-frontier-hackathon-track)
- **Why it misses:** 100xDevs is Harkirat Singh’s educational cohort. Unless you are an active student in that specific cohort, this is likely an exclusive track for his alumni.
- **Verdict:** Skip, unless you are an alumni.
