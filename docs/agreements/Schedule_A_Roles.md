# SCHEDULE A: Roles & Responsibilities

This document serves as an attachment to the **Vela Protocol Master Founding Team Agreement**. It outlines the core expectations and deliverables for each of the five (5) Founding Team members.

---

### 1. Product Director (Michael)
**Focus:** Executive Leadership, Product Vision, and Distribution
* Drives the overarching vision, roadmap, and business strategy of Vela Protocol.
* Manages all community engagement, distribution strategies, and external partnerships.
* Has final approval authority on all product designs, technical architecture, and marketing copy.
* Responsible for pitching to investors, securing grants (e.g., Colosseum), and managing the 45% Vela Treasury for business reinvestment.

### 2. Product Designer
**Focus:** User Experience (UX) and User Interface (UI)
* Responsible for designing the core `vela.finance` landing page and the decentralized application (dApp) dashboard.
* Creates wireframes, high-fidelity mockups (Figma), and interactive prototypes.
* Ensures the product interface is highly intuitive, institutional-grade, and aligns with the defined brand identity.
* Collaborates directly with the engineering team to ensure pixel-perfect implementation of UI designs.

### 3. Technical Lead (Marvin)
**Focus:** Smart Contracts and Protocol Engineering
* Architects and develops the core Solana smart contracts (using Anchor/Rust).
* Designs the security architecture, including math overflow guards and oracle staleness protections.
* Oversees the implementation of the Next.js frontend and the off-chain Keeper infrastructure.
* Responsible for technical audits, managing GitHub repositories, and ensuring the protocol can safely handle user funds.

### 4. Visual Designer / Illustrator
**Focus:** Brand Identity and Marketing Assets
* Designs high-quality, editorial-grade visual assets for Vela Protocol’s social media channels (X/Twitter threads, blogs, and articles).
* Creates pitch deck graphics, banners, and profile pictures ensuring strict adherence to Vela's brand guidelines (e.g., deep navy background, cyan accents, sailboat logo).
* Develops custom illustrations (e.g., comic strips, infographics) to visually explain complex DeFi concepts to the community.

### 5. Content Manager / Writer
**Focus:** Communications, Copywriting, and Research
* Authors long-form content, pitch deck copy, Hackathon submission text, and market research reports.
* Drafts high-engagement X (Twitter) threads, product announcements, and educational blogs.
* Ensures all external communication matches the "institutional but relentlessly resourceful" tone of Vela Protocol.
* Collaborates with the Visual Designer to pair written content with compelling graphics.
