## 2. Revenue & Funding Distribution
In the event that the Project successfully reaches a revenue-generating stage or secures external capital (e.g., hackathon grants, VC funding, protocol fees, or ecosystem grants), all inbound capital shall be distributed strictly according to the following model:

* **11%** — Distributed to the Product Director
* **11%** — Distributed to the Product Designer
* **11%** — Distributed to the Technical Lead
* **11%** — Distributed to the Visual Designer
* **11%** — Distributed to the Content Manager
* **45%** — Retained in the **Vela Treasury**

**The Vela Treasury (45%)**
The 45% retained in the Vela Treasury shall be utilized exclusively for reinvestment into the business. This includes, but is not limited to: smart contract security audits, marketing campaigns, liquidity provisioning, infrastructure costs, and future hiring. Disbursement of Treasury funds requires the approval of the Product Director.

---

## 3. Team Security & The "No-Fire" Clause
Vela Protocol operates on a principle of absolute loyalty and job security for the Founding Team.

**3.1. Protection from Termination:**
No member of the Founding Team shall be laid off, fired, or involuntarily terminated from the Project, both during the founding stage and in perpetuity, provided they continue to execute the responsibilities outlined in Schedule A. 

**3.2. Voluntary Resignation:**
The only condition under which a Founding Team member's tenure with Vela Protocol ends is if they voluntarily choose to resign or formally notify the team that they no longer wish to continue working on the Project.

---

## 4. Resignation Policy & Revenue Forfeiture
**4.1. Final Distribution:**
If a Founding Team member voluntarily resigns or ceases to contribute to the Project, they shall receive their 11% revenue share distribution *only* for the calendar month in which their resignation is officially tendered.

**4.2. Forfeiture of Future Revenue:**
Following the final calendar month of their resignation, the departing member fully forfeits any claim to future revenue distributions, grants, or treasury disbursements. 

**4.3. Reallocation:**
Upon a member's resignation, their 11% allocation shall automatically revert to the Vela Treasury (increasing the Treasury allocation to 56%) until a suitable replacement is hired, at which point a new agreement will be drafted.

---

## 5. Intellectual Property (IP) Assignment
To ensure the Project remains legally unencumbered and attractive to external investors, all Founding Team members agree to the following:

Any code, designs, artwork, branding, smart contracts, marketing copy, architectures, or other intellectual property (collectively, "IP") created by a Founding Team member for Vela Protocol during their tenure is considered a "work made for hire." 

All rights, title, and interest to this IP are permanently assigned to Vela Protocol (and its future corporate entity). No individual team member may withhold IP, issue takedown notices, or claim individual copyright over work submitted to the Project, even in the event of their voluntary resignation.
